Meeting recorders for Mac, compared

Updated 31 October 2026

Otter and Fireflies send a bot into your calls; Granola made bot-free notes popular. If you want meeting notes on a Mac without a bot, a cloud account or a subscription, these are the options — including when each of the others is still the right call.

How to choose

  • You need a notetaker that attends meetings you skip → Otter or Fireflies; only a bot can do that.
  • You need Windows, a phone app or a team workspace → Granola, or Meetily if you want it open source.
  • You want an open-source notepad you type into, with sync → Anarlog.
  • You want everything on your own machine with any local model → Meetily with Ollama.
  • You're on a Mac and use Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex → Overheard: your meetings become something Claude can search and quote.

Each comparison links the pages its facts come from. Prices and plans change; check each product's site before deciding. Overheard is not affiliated with any of the products compared here.
