Updated 22 October 2026

How to choose

  • You need Windows, a phone app or a team workspace → Granola, or Meetily if you want it open source.
  • You want everything on your own machine with any local model → Meetily with Ollama.
  • You're on a Mac and use Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex → Overheard: your meetings become something Claude can search and quote.

Each comparison links the pages its facts come from. Prices and plans change; check each product's site before deciding. Overheard is not affiliated with Granola or Meetily.
