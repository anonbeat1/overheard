Overheard vs Fireflies.ai

Checked against Fireflies' own pages on 31 October 2026

Fireflies.ai is a cloud meeting assistant built around a bot, the Fireflies Notetaker, that joins your calls; paid plans add a desktop app that can record without it. Overheard is a free, open-source Mac app that only ever records from your Mac and keeps every meeting as files Claude can read.

Pick Fireflies if…

you need a team workspace with analytics and CRM integrations, Windows or phone apps, a bot that attends meetings you skip, or SSO and compliance for a company rollout.

Pick Overheard if…

you're on a Mac, want bot-free recording without paying per seat, want your meetings on your own disk, and use Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Codex.

Where Fireflies is better

Built for teams. Fireflies is made for sales and customer teams: shared channels, conversation analytics, CRM and Zapier integrations, and SSO, SCIM and audit logs on Enterprise. Overheard has none of that — it's one person's recorder.

A notetaker that attends for you. The Fireflies bot can join a meeting you aren't in. Overheard records only what plays on your Mac.

Every platform. Fireflies works in the browser, on Windows and on phones. Overheard is Mac-only.

Where Overheard is better

Bot-free by design, and free. With Fireflies, recording without the bot means the desktop app on a paid seat. Overheard has only one mode: it records the call from your Mac with Core Audio process taps, your voice on its own track, and it costs nothing. How the recording works.

Your meetings stay yours. Every meeting is a folder in ~/Meetings with the audio, transcript and summary as plain files. With on-device transcription the audio never leaves your Mac — no vendor retention policy to read.

Claude without rate limits. Overheard's MCP server runs on your Mac and reads your files directly, so Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Codex can search every meeting as often as you like. There's also a Claude Code plugin with recap, follow-up and to-do commands. Read how it works.

No AI credits. Summaries and chat use the Claude Code or Codex subscription you already have, or Apple Intelligence on-device.

Switching

Overheard starts from your next call. If you have audio files of older meetings, drop them into Overheard's Import to have them transcribed and searchable alongside the new ones. You can run both side by side while you decide.
